To keep your bag looking its best:
  • Wipe clean with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth after each use
  • Keep away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and humidity
  • Avoid contact with cosmetics, perfumes, and alcohol-based products
  • Store in a dust bag or pillowcase when not in use
  • Stuff loosely with tissue paper to help the bag hold its shape in storage
  • For deep stains, consult a professional leather care specialist
